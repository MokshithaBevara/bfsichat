# AgentOps – BFSI AI Chatbot

## Overview

AgentOps is an AI-powered BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance) chatbot developed during EY Techathon 6.0.

The project was designed to provide users with quick, intelligent, and context-aware assistance for banking-related queries. By leveraging Large Language Models and conversational AI, the chatbot helps users access financial information, understand banking services, and receive personalized guidance through a simple chat interface.

Our team successfully advanced to Round 2 of EY Techathon 6.0 with this solution.

---

## Problem Statement

Customers often struggle to find relevant banking information quickly through traditional support channels. Long wait times, complex documentation, and limited accessibility can negatively impact customer experience.

Our goal was to build an AI-powered assistant capable of delivering instant, accurate, and user-friendly responses to common banking and financial queries.

---

AgentOps provides:
